## Reading the animations

These maps combine historical evidence with explicit visualization models. A reconstructed route, broad date interval or estimated population is not an independently documented itinerary, exact departure year or census. Source traditions and conflicting interpretations are discussed in the edition documentation. Colours and geographic envelopes organize the display; they should not be read as modern borders or a complete ethnic census.

Dates on the timeline are historical model dates. Playback speed controls presentation time, not historical travel speed. Editions may use different chronological phase boundaries and rates. The published video schedule is described in each edition's documentation; it must not be transferred from one atlas to another.

## Original materials and publication

The original user-supplied research, project prompts and private working files are retained separately in the private archive. Public documentation identifies historical references and modelling decisions; it does not publish those private originals or make private links usable. The public website and the private archive are separate publication surfaces.
